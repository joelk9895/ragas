As an applied research engineer at Ragas, you’ll tackle complex challenges in machine learning by skillfully leveraging open research to devise innovative solutions.

Some of the work will include

- Developing optimization algorithms to align LLM-based evaluators with human assessments.
- Creating algorithms that synthesize test data samples for evaluating LLM applications, such as RAG and tool-use workflows.
- Fine-tuning and aligning LLMs to function as custom evaluators.

You may be a good fit if

- You have a keen interest in reading research, identifying patterns, and striking a balance between critical evaluation and openness to novel solutions.
- You have worked extensively with LLMs, synthetic data generation and optimisation.
- You enjoy and have contributed in open-source and Kaggle.

We believe the next wave of engineers building LLM applications deserve the best tools to help bring their ideas to the world.
